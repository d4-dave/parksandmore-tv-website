Disney’s 2026 D23 Parks Showcase Will Stream Live — Here’s What We’re Watching For

August 10, 2026

Disney fans won’t have to be inside the Honda Center to follow one of D23’s biggest presentations this year.

Disney has confirmed that Horizons: Disney Experiences Showcase will stream live on Disney+ on Saturday, August 15 at 7:00 p.m. PT / 10:00 p.m. ET.

The showcase is part of a larger lineup of D23 presentations streaming throughout the weekend.

See the full D23 Disney+ streaming schedule →

D23 is also framing this year’s show as a deeper look at the ambitious projects announced in 2024. Host Neil Patrick Harris will guide fans through behind-the-scenes looks, conversations with Imagineers and creative leaders, project updates, performances, announcements, and a few surprises along the way.

That makes this year’s presentation especially interesting for Walt Disney World fans, because several major projects are already in active development.

What we expect to hear more about

Piston Peak National Park

Disney has already said that Piston Peak National Park will become part of the largest expansion in Magic Kingdom history.

The Cars-inspired area is being designed as a new part of Frontierland, with wilderness-inspired storytelling, national-park-style architecture, and a major off-road rally experience.

We would be surprised if D23 passed without another look at how the project is developing.

Villains Land

Villains Land remains one of the most anticipated Walt Disney World projects on the horizon.

Disney has already confirmed that the land will include two major attractions, dining, and shopping, all built around a darker Disney Villains concept beyond Big Thunder Mountain Railroad.

D23 would be a natural place for attraction details, updated artwork, or a better look at what guests will actually experience.

Monstropolis at Disney’s Hollywood Studios

Monstropolis is also taking shape, and Disney says steel is already rising behind the construction walls.

The official Disney Parks Blog has shared new story details around H.U.M.A.N. Day, the city’s invitation for humans to enter the monster world.

With construction underway and Disney’s first suspended coaster planned for the land, Monstropolis feels like another strong candidate for new footage, attraction details, or updated concept art during Horizons.

A few things we’d love to hear

Then there are the announcements that may be less likely — but would definitely get our attention.

We’d love to see Disney:

  • Bring the traditional Magic Kingdom park opening back to the Main Street train station, restoring the pre-2017 welcome that started the day outside the park before guests entered Main Street, U.S.A.
  • Revisit the timeline of Star Wars: Galaxy’s Edge, bringing more of the original trilogy into the land and making classic characters and stories a bigger part of the experience.
  • Add more meaningful on-property hotel perks at Walt Disney World, giving resort guests a stronger day-to-day advantage and a little more of that “staying in the bubble” feeling.

None of those have been announced. They’re simply on our Parks & More wish list.

And one rumor we’ll be listening for…

There has also been ongoing speculation that Space Mountain could eventually close for a major rebuild or retracking project.

Disney has not announced that, and there is no confirmed closure, so this stays firmly in rumor territory for now.

Still, if Tomorrowland or Space Mountain comes up during Horizons, a lot of fans will be paying attention.

Whatever Disney has planned, the fact that the showcase is streaming live should make this year’s D23 much easier for Disney Parks fans to follow in real time.
