Disney Announces 2026 EPCOT Candlelight Processional Narrator Lineup

September 8, 2026

Disney has announced the 2026 celebrity narrator lineup for the Candlelight Processional at EPCOT, including several new names joining returning favorites for this year’s holiday season.

The Candlelight Processional returns as part of the EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays, which begins November 27.

New narrators for 2026 include:

  • Robin Roberts — December 6–7
  • Jason Kelce — December 8–9
  • Lacey Chabert — December 22–23

Returning narrators include Auli‘i Cravalho, Gary Sinise, Whoopi Goldberg, Neil Patrick Harris, Lauren Daigle, Ralph Macchio, Henry Winkler, Jodi Benson, Simu Liu, and Eva Longoria.

2026 Candlelight Processional Narrator Schedule

  • November 27–28 — Auli‘i Cravalho
  • November 29–December 1 — Gary Sinise
  • December 2–3 — To Be Announced
  • December 4–5 — Whoopi Goldberg
  • December 6–7 — Robin Roberts
  • December 8–9 — Jason Kelce
  • December 10–12 — Neil Patrick Harris
  • December 13–14 — To Be Announced
  • December 15–16 — Lauren Daigle
  • December 17–19 — Ralph Macchio
  • December 20–21 — Henry Winkler
  • December 22–23 — Lacey Chabert
  • December 24–25 — Jodi Benson
  • December 26–27 — Simu Liu
  • December 28–30 — Eva Longoria

Performances are scheduled nightly at 4:45 PM, 6:30 PM, and 8:00 PM at the America Gardens Theatre in EPCOT.

The Candlelight Processional combines a celebrity narrator with the Voices of Liberty, a live orchestra, and a large choir to retell the Christmas story as part of one of Walt Disney World’s longest-running holiday traditions.

Disney says the production is celebrating its 55th year at Walt Disney World Resort and its 32nd year at EPCOT in 2026.

Candlelight Processional Dining Packages

Candlelight Processional Dining Packages will return for 2026, offering a meal at select EPCOT restaurants along with reserved seating for one of the day’s performances.

Booking is scheduled to begin October 14.
